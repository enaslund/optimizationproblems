# Furstenberg–Sárközy exponent for square-difference-free sets

## Description of constant

Let $r(N)$ be the maximum size of a subset $A\subset\\{1,\dots,N\\}$ with no non-zero square differences $a-b=n^2$. Then $C\_{4b}$ is the least constant such that $r(N) \leq N^{C\_{4b}+o(1)}$.

## Known lower bounds

| Bound | Reference | Comments |
| ----- | --------- | -------- |
| $\tfrac12$ | Trivial / folklore (see [BG2008]) | Can use an arithmetic progression of spacing $p \asymp \sqrt{N}$ |
| $\frac12\!\left(1+\frac{\log 7}{\log 65}\right)\approx 0.733077$ | [Ruz1984] | Base-expansion construction |
| $\frac12\!\left(1+\frac{\log 12}{\log 205}\right)\approx 0.733412$ | [Lew2015] | Improves modulus and residue set in base expansion |
| $\frac{37903373}{50000000}=0.75806746$ | [Nas2026] | Interval-moment construction with six prime chains, two composite digit alphabets and a binary recursion. Square bases: $3^2,7^2,11^2,31^2,59^2,103^2,215^6,437^6,4^{10^{10}}$; the two odd depth-three witnesses have $4913$ and $19683$ points. The nine rational moment powers sum to $0.758067485671$, exceeding the certified exponent by $0.000000025671$. Full Lean proof, registered with Palomar; see below. |

## Additional comments and links

- Many non-trivial upper bounds on $r(N)$ [F1977],[Sar1978],[PSS1988],[BM2021],[GS2025], but unfortunately these bounds have not yet improved the trivial upper bound $C\_{4b} \leq 1$.
- The limit $\lim_{N\to\infty} \frac{\log r(N)}{\log N}$ is conjectured to exist [Ruz1984].  This remains open.
- Extensions to other polynomials are discussed in [Rice2019].
- A survey of this problem can be found in [Wol2005].

- [Nas2026] proves that, for every $\varepsilon>0$ and all sufficiently large $N$, $r(N)\geq N^{0.75806746-\varepsilon}$, hence $C\_{4b}\geq 0.75806746$. This is the exact certified exponent, not a rounded numerical estimate of a limiting value.
- **Formal verification of [Nas2026].** [Palomar entry PALOMAR-2026-09-19-000006, version 1](https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-09-19-000006&version=1), registered 19 September 2026, records the general interval-moment criterion and `SarkozySubmission.improved_bound` at commit [`e5d693729e23762b063a55015ad79ccaf28a3217`](https://github.com/enaslund/sarkozy-lower-bound-0.758/tree/e5d693729e23762b063a55015ad79ccaf28a3217). Its [verification run](https://github.com/PalomarRegistry/PalomarSubmission/actions/runs/35452774924) checks the submitted statements with Comparator, Lean's kernel and independent NanoDa replay; the permitted axioms are `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`. All nine components, their finite certificates and the asymptotic deduction are included, with no remaining certificate hypotheses. The repository also provides a [self-contained paper](https://github.com/enaslund/sarkozy-lower-bound-0.758/blob/e5d693729e23762b063a55015ad79ccaf28a3217/papers/square-difference-free-sets-of-exponent-0.75806746.pdf) and an [exact Python certificate program](https://github.com/enaslund/sarkozy-lower-bound-0.758/blob/e5d693729e23762b063a55015ad79ccaf28a3217/papers/square-difference-free-certificate.py).
